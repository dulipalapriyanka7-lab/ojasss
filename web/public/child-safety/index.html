Child Safety Standards

OJASSS (package com.ojasss.inida) · Last updated 22 November 2026

OJASSS has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behaviour is prohibited on OJASSS, is removed, and leads to a permanent ban and a report to the authorities.

1. Who can use OJASSS

OJASSS is only for users aged 18 and over. Accounts that we find or believe belong to minors are suspended.

2. What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, including images, videos, drawings, AI-generated or edited media, text and links.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation of a minor, sextortion, or any attempt to contact a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, selling or trading child sexual abuse material, or linking to it.
  • Content that promotes, normalises or glorifies the abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Trafficking or any commercial sexual exploitation of children.

3. How to report

Every post, video, comment, live room and profile in the app has a Report option. Choose the child-safety reason, and our moderation team reviews those reports with top priority. You can also block any user immediately.

You can also email our child-safety contact at kvkmsolutions@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report". Do not attach the abusive material itself; send the link or username instead.

4. What we do

  • Our moderators review reported content and chats, and remove violating content as quickly as possible.
  • We permanently ban offending accounts and stop them from creating new ones.
  • We preserve relevant evidence and report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the NCMEC CyberTipline where applicable, as required by law.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement requests.

5. Compliance with law

OJASSS complies with applicable child-safety laws, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules in India, and other applicable laws.

6. Child-safety contact

Our designated point of contact for CSAE matters, including contact from Google Play and law enforcement, is: kvkmsolutions@gmail.com.
